# My Favorite Series: *Ghost Doctor*  

![Ghost Doctor](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/f/f4/Ghost_Doctor_TV_series_poster.jpg)

---

## 🩺 Overview  

**Ghost Doctor** is a South Korean fantasy medical drama that tells the story of two doctors—one arrogant genius and one kind but inexperienced—whose souls accidentally combine due to a supernatural incident. Together, they must save patients while learning lessons about life, humility, and compassion.  

The show perfectly mixes **fantasy**, **comedy**, and **medical drama**, creating a touching yet humorous story about what it truly means to be a doctor.  

---

## Main Characters  

- **Cha Young Min (Rain / Jung Ji-hoon)** – A highly skilled but arrogant cardiothoracic surgeon who becomes a ghost after a car accident.  
- **Go Seung Tak (Kim Bum)** – A genius medical intern from a wealthy family who becomes possessed by Young Min’s spirit.  
- **Jang Se-jin (Uee)** – A neurosurgeon and Young Min’s ex-girlfriend who still cares deeply for him.  
- **Oh Soo-jung (Son Na-eun)** – An emergency room intern who believes in supernatural phenomena and helps Young Min.  

---

## My Favorite Things About It  

1. The **unique mix of comedy, fantasy, and hospital drama** 🩻  
2. The **character development** between Young Min and Seung Tak  
3. The **heartwarming story** about teamwork and redemption  
4. The **emotional scenes** that show the value of saving lives  
5. The **perfect chemistry** between Rain and Kim Bum  

> “A true doctor doesn’t save lives for recognition… but because it’s their calling.” – *Cha Young Min*
